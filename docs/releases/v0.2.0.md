# BunnyBar v0.2.0 🐇

The first official release of **BunnyBar**: a tiny pixel-art bunny that lives above your action bar in **World of Warcraft: Forever**. It hops around, blinks, takes naps, and can wear any fur colour you like, with matching bunny ears on your portrait.

BunnyBar is purely decorative. It doesn't automate anything, read combat data or touch your action buttons.

## ✨ What's in this release

### The bunny
- **Animated pixel art**: an original bunny that hops along its own little track with smooth, arcing jumps. It blinks, twitches its ears and breathes while it waits.
- **Naps**: every now and then it gets sleepy, yawns, curls up and dozes with a drifting "z". Then it wakes up, stretches and gets back to hopping.
- **Finds its own spot**: it sits just above the highest visible bar at the bottom of your screen, whether that's Action Bar 2, the XP or reputation bar, or the main action bar.
- **Live repositioning**: toggle an action bar, hit the level cap or rearrange things in Edit Mode, and the bunny moves by itself. No `/reload` needed.
- **Put it anywhere**: `/bunny unlock`, drag it, `/bunny lock`. Your spot is remembered.

### Make it yours
- **Settings panel** under **Esc → Options → AddOns → BunnyBar**, or type `/bunny options`.
- **Any fur colour**: pick it from a colour square with hue and brightness sliders, or type a hex code such as `#FFB6C1` (pink) or `#87CEEB` (sky blue). Only the fur changes; the eyes, nose, pink inner ears and outline stay put.
- **Five sizes**: Tiny 16, Small 32, Regular 64 (the default), Large 96 and Huge 128. Prefer something in between? `/bunny size 48` works too.

### Portrait accessories
- **Bunny ears** on your own portrait and on your party members' portraits, with a separate on/off switch for each.
- The ears always **match your bunny's fur colour**.
- They follow your portraits through **Edit Mode** moves and stay in front of the portrait frame.
- They're local and cosmetic only: nobody else sees them, they're click-through, and BunnyBar never changes Blizzard's unit frames.

## 📦 Installation

1. Download **`BunnyBar-0.2.0.zip`** below.
2. Extract it into your WoW Forever AddOns folder, so that you end up with `AddOns/BunnyBar/BunnyBar.toc`:
   - **Windows:** `C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`
   - **macOS:** `/Applications/World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`
3. **Fully restart** the game. A plain `/reload` may not pick up a new addon.
4. On the character select screen, open **AddOns** and make sure **BunnyBar** is ticked.

Need more detail, like creating a missing `AddOns` folder or finding it via the Battle.net launcher? See the [installation guide](https://github.com/aivarnit/BunnyBar/blob/v0.2.0/docs/INSTALLATION.md).

**Upgrading from a development build?** Your settings are kept, including position, size, speed and visibility. New options start at their defaults.

To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with `BunnyBar-0.2.0.zip.sha256`, attached below.

## ⌨️ Slash commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/bunny` | Show all commands |
| `/bunny options` | Open the settings panel |
| `/bunny color #FFB6C1` | Set the fur colour (`/bunny color reset` for the original) |
| `/bunny size 64` | Set the size (16–128; presets 16, 32, 64, 96, 128) |
| `/bunny ears player on\|off` | Bunny ears on your portrait |
| `/bunny ears party on\|off` | Bunny ears on party portraits |
| `/bunny show` / `hide` | Show or hide the bunny |
| `/bunny unlock` / `lock` | Drag the bunny / lock it in place |
| `/bunny reset` | Restore all defaults |
| `/bunny test` | Make it hop right now |
| `/bunny speed`, `interval`, `width`, `height` | Fine-tune hopping |
| `/bunny stats`, `/bunny bars` | Status and troubleshooting info |

Full reference: [COMMANDS.md](https://github.com/aivarnit/BunnyBar/blob/v0.2.0/docs/COMMANDS.md)

## 🧩 Compatibility

- **Designed and tested for World of Warcraft: Forever** (interface `16001`) on Windows and macOS.
- Retail, Classic Era and other clients have **not** been tested.
- No other addons or libraries are required.

## ⚠️ Known limitations

- Portrait ears need **Blizzard's default unit frames**. Raid-style party frames have no portraits, and replacement unit-frame addons aren't supported yet.
- Automatic placement looks for **Blizzard's default action bars and XP bar**. If it can't find them, the bunny sits a little above the bottom of the screen, and you can drag it wherever you like.
- In Edit Mode, the ears catch up with a portrait when you **release** it, not while you're still dragging.
- At the **Tiny** size, the tiniest details (eye shine, mouth) can disappear. Sizes 32, 64, 96 and 128 are pixel-perfect at UI scale 1.
- If a future WoW Forever patch marks BunnyBar as *out of date*, tick **Load out of date AddOns** until an update is out.
- Settings are shared across your whole account (no per-character profiles yet).
